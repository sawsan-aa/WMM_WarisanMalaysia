The art of defense is the rhythm of the spirit.

Silat is more than martial arts; it is a physical philosophy of grace, respect, and Malay heritage. Every movement tells a story of courage and discipline, preserved through generations and performed during cultural celebrations across Malaysia.

Wayang Kulit

Wayang Kulit is one of Malaysia's oldest performing arts, combining storytelling, music, craftsmanship and shadow puppetry. Every performance is guided by a skilled puppeteer known as the Dalang, who brings legendary characters to life through light and shadow.
