Spiritual Sites

Sites of culture and history, where the sacred and the profane meet in a living synthesis of faith, tradition, and community.

First Location

Batu Caves  –  Gombak, Selangor

A giant Hindu statue at the forefront of a rainbow staircase leading to Hindu temples inside limestone caves. It is one of the most well-known must see places in Kuala Lumpur.

Second Location

Thean Hou Temple  –  Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

This ornate Chinese temple dedicated to the Queen of Heaven, Thean Hou, protectorate of people who make their living from the sea. The temple borrows from Buddhist, Taoist and Confucian traditions. Some visitors believe the panoramic views of KL alone make the trip up the hill worthwhile.

Third Location

Putra Mosque  –  Putrajaya, Malaysia

The Masjid Putra or Putra Mosque is the principal mosque located at the heart of the federal territory of Putrajaya, Malaysia. Calm, welcoming, and serene, The architecture is a perfect blend of modern design and traditional Islamic art, crowned by a pink dome reflected over a clear waters.
