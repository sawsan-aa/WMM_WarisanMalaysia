MELAKA TOWN

"Where the Empire Began"

Once the crown of a flourishing maritime empire, Melaka's storied straits drew Portuguese, Dutch, and British fleets across four centuries. Its UNESCO old town preserves a rare layering of Malay sultanate grandeur, European colonial forts, and vibrant Peranakan shophouses.

HISTORY NOTES :

Founded circa 1400 by Parameswara, Melaka grew into the greatest entrepôt of the medieval Asian world, controlling spice routes linking India to China.

CULINARY SIGNATURE :

Nyonya cuisine is the city's soul: asam pedas fish, chicken kapitan, and the iconic cendol crowned with gula Melaka palm sugar syrup.
